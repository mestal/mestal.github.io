Support

Pixel Bloom is a nonogram puzzle game. If you have a question, found a bug or need help, we are happy to help.

Email: mestal@gmail.com

We usually reply within a few business days.

To help us solve your issue faster, please include:

  • Your device model and iOS version,
  • The App version,
  • A short description of the problem and, if possible, a screenshot.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the game need an account or an internet connection?

No account is needed. Your progress is stored on your device. Ads and the optional rewarded ads need an internet connection.

Can I move my progress to a new device?

Progress is saved only on your device and is not backed up to our servers, so it cannot be transferred. If you restore your device from an iCloud or computer backup, the game data is restored with it.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a player slot inside the App (press and hold the slot) or uninstall the App. We hold no data about you. See the Privacy Policy.

How do the controls work?

Tap a square to fill it. Hold and slide to fill many squares. Double tap to place an X (double tap an X again to remove it). The first five levels are a step-by-step tutorial, and the Hint button explains the next logical move.

I ran out of lives or hints.

You can watch an optional rewarded ad to get +1 life or +1 hint, or restart the level.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 24, 2026

Mesut Alpaslan ("Developer", "We") has developed and published the Turkish Rummy: 101 application ("App"). This page informs the people using the App ("User", "You") about which personal data is collected, how it is used, with whom it is shared, how long it is kept and how it can be deleted. This text also serves as the information notice under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK").

1. Data Controller

Your personal data is processed by Mesut Alpaslan as the data controller. Contact: mestal@gmail.com

2. Data We Collect

  • Account Information: When you first open the App, a guest account and a unique user ID are created for you automatically. If you link your account with Google, Apple or Facebook, the email address, full name and profile photo provided by that provider are stored. When signing in with Apple, you can choose to hide your email address.
  • Profile and Game Data: Username, selected avatar, point balance, number of games played and game results, game history, language preference, table theme and your game preferences.
  • Social Data: Friend list, friend requests, users you have blocked, gifts you have sent and your invitation setting.
  • Chat and Reports: In-game chat messages are not stored permanently on our servers. When a user submits a report, the latest chat messages at the table are stored with the report as evidence.
  • Contact Us Form: The email address and message you submit through the form.
  • Purchase Information: Transaction records of your in-app point purchases (product, amount, date, transaction ID). Your payment details (card information etc.) are processed by Google Play or the App Store and are not shared with us.
  • Technical Data: Device platform (Android, iOS, Web), app version, last connection time and a daily usage record (the days on which you opened the App). Your IP address is processed to establish the connection to our servers and to prevent abuse, but it is not stored permanently.
  • Error Logs: When an error occurs in the App, the technical details of the error, the app version and platform information are sent to our servers.
  • Advertising Data: To show rewarded ads, our ad provider Google AdMob may use your device's advertising ID. On iOS, ad tracking only takes place if you grant permission.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To create your account, keep you signed in and let you use your account on different devices,
  • To run the multiplayer game, match players and keep points and statistics,
  • To provide social features such as friends, invitations, chat and gifts,
  • To review reports, prevent rule violations and keep the service safe,
  • To verify purchases and ad rewards and to handle refunds and fraud,
  • To fix errors and to measure and improve service quality,
  • To respond to your support requests.

Your data is processed on the legal grounds set out in Article 5 of KVKK: where it is necessary for the conclusion or performance of a contract, for compliance with legal obligations, and for our legitimate interests provided that your fundamental rights and freedoms are not harmed. We do not sell your data.

4. Third-Party Services and International Transfers

The App uses the following service providers for its core functions. As the servers of these services may be located abroad, your data may be transferred outside Türkiye in order to provide these services:

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Sign in with Google, Google Play Services, AdMob)
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, App Store)
  • Meta (Facebook Login)
  • RevenueCat (in-app purchase infrastructure)
  • Cloud server and database providers (game server and data storage)

5. Retention Periods

  • Your account, profile, game and social data is kept as long as your account is active.
  • Daily usage records are deleted automatically after 1 year.
  • Error logs are deleted automatically after at most 90 days (10 days by default).
  • Pending friend requests are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • The periods that apply when an account is deleted are described in the "Account and Data Deletion" section below.

6. Account and Data Deletion

In the App: You can delete an account linked with Google, Apple or Facebook at any time via Settings > "Delete Account". When you delete your account:

  • Your account and sign-in record (Firebase) are deleted immediately; the account can no longer be signed in to.
  • Your friend list, your entries in other users' lists, your friend requests, your daily usage records and your Contact Us form messages are deleted.
  • If you signed in with Apple, the App's authorization in your Apple account is revoked. If you signed in with Google, the App's access in your Google account is removed in the mobile app.
  • To resolve support requests and possible disputes, a copy of your account record is kept for at most 30 days and is permanently deleted at the end of this period.
  • Purchase and ad reward transaction records continue to be kept due to legal obligations and refund and fraud checks. Game records that form part of other players' game history, and reports made about you or by you, may be kept for safety purposes.

Outside the App: If you cannot access the App, use a guest account, or want to request data deletion via Facebook, you can fill in the deletion request form or send an email to mestal@gmail.com. Your request will be completed within 30 days at the latest.

Important: Points and purchased virtual items in a deleted account cannot be restored and are not refunded.

7. Your Rights

Under Article 11 of KVKK, you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, to request information about it, to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly, to know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred, to request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate, to request its deletion or destruction where the conditions are met, and to claim compensation for damages arising from unlawful processing. You can send your requests to mestal@gmail.com.

8. User-Generated Content (UGC) and Moderation

Profanity, hate speech, harassment, sexual content and sharing illegal activities in the in-game chat are strictly prohibited. Automatic filters are used to block inappropriate language. Users can report rule-breaking behavior from within the App and block users they do not want to interact with. The Developer reserves the right to review reported content and to apply sanctions such as warnings, chat restrictions or temporary or permanent account suspension. See the Membership Agreement for details.

9. Cookies and Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores settings such as your sound, language and game preferences only on your device. The App itself does not use advertising cookies; however, the third-party services it uses (for example, sign-in services in the web version) may use their own cookies.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures such as encrypted connections and access restrictions to protect your data. However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or of electronic storage is 100% secure.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware of such a case, we will delete the data immediately. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always published on this page and the "Last Updated" date at the top is changed.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 19, 2026

Mesut Alpaslan ("Developer", "We") has developed and published the Pixel Bloom application ("App"), a nonogram puzzle game. This page explains what information the App handles. In short: the developer does not collect or store any personal data on its own servers. The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which may process some device data as described in section 4.

1. Developer and Contact

Mesut Alpaslan. Contact: mestal@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information. The App has no user accounts, no sign-in, no chat, and no social features. We do not use our own analytics or crash-reporting services (the ad provider described in section 4 may process limited technical data), and we do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

Your game progress is saved only on your device: player slot names and avatar colors you choose, completed levels, stars, scores, remaining hints, and settings such as language and sound. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the App deletes it, and you can delete a player slot inside the App at any time.

4. Internet Access and Third-Party Services

  • Fonts: The App may download its display font from Google Fonts when it runs. As with any internet request, Google receives technical information such as your IP address. We do not receive or store this information.
  • Advertising: The App shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC): a full-screen ad between some levels and optional rewarded video ads that you can choose to watch to earn an extra life or hint. To serve, measure and protect ads against fraud, AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, where your device settings allow it), your IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad interaction data and technical diagnostics. We do not receive this data. How Google handles it is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can limit ad personalization or reset your advertising identifier in your device settings (on iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security; on Android: Settings > Google > Ads).

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone other than the ad provider described above, which receives data directly from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App has no in-app purchases, no links to external websites in gameplay, and no way to communicate with other people.

6. Data Deletion

Since all data is stored on your device, you can delete it by deleting your player slots in the App or by uninstalling the App. We hold no data about you on our servers. Data processed by the ad provider is controlled by that provider; see its privacy policy above.

7. Security

Your game data stays in the App's private storage on your device and is protected by the Android and iOS operating systems.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always published on this page and the "Last Updated" date at the top is changed.

9. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy: mestal@gmail.com

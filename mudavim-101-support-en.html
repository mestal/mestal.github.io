Support

Turkish Rummy: 101 is a multiplayer 101 Okey (Turkish rummy) game. If you have a question, found a bug or need help with your account, we are happy to help.

Email: mestal@gmail.com

You can also reach us from inside the App: open the menu in the lobby and tap "Contact Us".

We usually reply within a few business days.

To help us solve your issue faster, please include:

  • Your username in the App,
  • Your device model and iOS version,
  • The App version (shown at the bottom of the start-up screen),
  • A short description of the problem and, if possible, a screenshot.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I keep my progress when I change devices?

When you first open the App, a guest account is created on your device. To keep your points and progress, link your account with Sign in with Apple, Google or Facebook (where available) and sign in with the same account on your new device. A guest account that is not linked cannot be moved to another device.

How do I delete my account?

If your account is linked with Apple, Google or Facebook, go to Settings > "Delete Account" in the App. If you use a guest account or cannot access the App, send us an email from the address you want handled and we will complete your request within 30 days. Points and virtual items in a deleted account cannot be restored. See the Privacy Policy for details on what is deleted and what is kept.

I lost my connection during a game.

Reopen the App as soon as possible; it will try to reconnect you to your table automatically. If the problem keeps happening, check your internet connection and make sure you are using the latest version of the App.

How do I report or block a player?

At the table, tap the player's profile and use "Report" to report inappropriate behavior or "Block" to stop seeing their messages. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. If you think your account was suspended by mistake, email us to appeal.

Does the App offer real-money gambling?

No. The App does not offer real-money gambling or any opportunity to win real money or prizes. Points cannot be exchanged for money, goods or services.
